# Necronomicon Providence 2026

There are many events over the long weekend that is Necronomicon Providence, and the information about those events is recorded across several sources, both HTML and PDF.

All sources have been saved here as either the original PDFs or as the web pages saved as PDF. Since all the Gaming programing is sign-up only, those events are not saved here (too late to sign up). Same goes for the ticketed Walking Tours (though their time slots are noted for reference, since they block out real time on the map).

# Data Sources

* Core Programming (along with a grid view)
* Extended Programming
* Armitage Symposium
* Film schedule

#### 🔵 Tainted and Impure: Environmental Contamination in the Weird
*Friday, 8:00a–9:30a · [OMNI-WN](#place-omni-wn)* [🐙](#row-fri-02 "Back to schedule")

A discussion on the feeling of "ick" and our response to real and imagined environmental contamination — how ideas of purity and pollution are entwined, and what they mean from a moral, literary, and scientific perspective. Panelists: Rick Claypool (Moderator), Chesya Burke, Eric Schaller, K. H. Vaughan, Mathilda Zeller.

<a id="fri-03"></a>
#### 🔵 Daphne du Maurier: A Strange Creature
*Friday, 8:00a–9:30a · [OMNI-BK](#place-omni-bk)* [🐙](#row-fri-03 "Back to schedule")

Du Maurier (English, 1907–1989) wrote broadly, including romantic, historical, adventure, and biography, often with an underlying hint of darkness or the supernatural, as in Don't Look Now, The Apple Tree, The Blue Lenses, and The Birds. Panelists discuss her work, controversies, and legacy. Panelists: Adam Golaski (Moderator), Victoria Dalpe, Robert Levy, Anya Martin.

<a id="fri-04"></a>
#### 🔵 Pre-Lovecraft Sources of Cosmic Horror
*Friday, 8:00a–9:30a · [GRAD-CAP](#place-grad-cap)* [🐙](#row-fri-04 "Back to schedule")

Practitioners, and even masters, of cosmic horror existed before Lovecraft, and were powerful influences on his work. Poe, Blackwood, Bierce, Machen, Chambers, and Dunsany are inevitably cited — but to what extent did HPL capture and solidify previous work, and at what point did his cosmic horror take on a life of its own? Panelists: Matthew Carpenter (Moderator), Leslie S. Klinger, Will Murray, Peter Rawlik.

<a id="fri-05"></a>
#### 🔵 A People's History of The Cthulhu Mythos: Leftist Appraisal and Reinterpretation
*Friday, 8:00a–9:30a · [GRAD-BILT](#place-grad-bilt)* [🐙](#row-fri-05 "Back to schedule")

Are colonialism, capitalism, and racism inherent in the foundation of the Mythos? What is the textual role of class in Lovecraft's work, and how have recent works attempted to reimagine or critique its underlying ideologies? Panelists: Edward Guimont (Moderator), Alexander Dickow, Khora Martel, Tonya Monteforte, Eric Williams.

#### 🔵 Theosophy and Weird Fiction: How Did the Esoterica of Madame Blavatsky Influence Cosmic Horror
